# Restatement note: net revenue version 2 (sample answer)

- Metric and glossary term: net_revenue, GT-0120
- Owner who approved the change: finance controller
- Change: version 2 deducts discounts as well as refunds from gross sales. Version 1 deducted refunds only.
- Effective from: 1 July 2026
- Reason: the statutory accounts report revenue after discounts, and the investor presentation has to agree with them.
- History: restated from 1 April 2025 for trend charts and for the investor presentation, so that quarter-on-quarter change compares like with like. Figures already published under version 1 are not withdrawn. They stay reproducible by asking for version 1.
- Size of the effect: for FY26-27 Q2, version 1 gives 681,090.62 and version 2 gives 643,813.12, a difference of 37,277.50.
- Who was told, and how long version 1 stays available: owners of the investor dashboard, the sales dashboard and the assistant's metric tool, by notice on 1 June 2026. Version 1 stays queryable until 31 March 2027 and is then deprecated.
