# Cheques: stop payment, returns and positive pay

## Stop payment
<!-- section: stop -->

A stop payment instruction can be given in the app, in internet banking, on the helpline or at a branch, before the cheque is presented. The charge is the one listed under service charges for the customer's account type.

## Returned cheques
<!-- section: returns -->

Table: charges when a cheque is returned
| Reason | Charge to the customer (INR) |
|---|---|
| Cheque issued by the customer returned for insufficient funds, first in a quarter | 350 |
| Cheque issued by the customer returned for insufficient funds, each further return in the quarter | 600 |
| Cheque deposited by the customer returned unpaid | 150 |
| Returned for a technical reason not attributable to the customer | nil |

## Positive pay
<!-- section: positivepay -->

For a cheque of INR 500,000 or more the customer must confirm the cheque number, date, amount and payee in the app or at a branch before it is presented. A cheque of that size presented without confirmation is returned.
