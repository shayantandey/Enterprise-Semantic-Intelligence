# Debit card daily limits: policy

## Daily limits
<!-- section: limits -->

Table: daily limits by debit card type, INR
| Card | ATM cash withdrawal | Purchases at shops and online |
|---|---|---|
| Classic debit card | 25,000 | 50,000 |
| Gold debit card | 50,000 | 150,000 |
| Platinum debit card | 100,000 | 500,000 |

The customer can set lower limits in the app at any time. Limits cannot be raised above the table.

## Use outside India
<!-- section: international -->

International use is switched off on every new card. The customer switches it on in the app or on the helpline, for a period of up to 90 days at a time.

## PIN
<!-- section: pin -->

A PIN is set by the customer in the app or at a Lakemont ATM using a one-time password. After 3 wrong attempts the card is blocked for ATM use until midnight.
