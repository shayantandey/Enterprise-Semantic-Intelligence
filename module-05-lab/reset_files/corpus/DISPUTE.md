# Card transaction disputes: process

## Raising a dispute
<!-- section: raise -->

A cardholder who does not recognise a transaction, or did not receive the goods or the cash, can raise a dispute within 60 days of the statement date. The agent records it on Form DSP-2 in the service system and gives the customer the reference number.

## Temporary credit
<!-- section: credit -->

For a disputed amount of up to INR 25,000 a temporary credit is given within 10 working days. For larger amounts the credit is given when the investigation supports the customer. The temporary credit is reversed if the dispute is decided for the merchant.

## Resolution time
<!-- section: timeline -->

A dispute is resolved within 90 days of the date it was raised. The customer is told the outcome by SMS and email.

## Unauthorised electronic transactions
<!-- section: liability -->

When the customer reports an unauthorised transaction within 3 working days of the bank's alert, the customer's liability is zero and the amount is credited within 10 working days.

Table: maximum customer liability when the report is made 4 to 7 working days after the alert
| Account or card type | Maximum liability (INR) |
|---|---|
| Basic savings account | 5,000 |
| Other savings accounts and credit cards with a limit up to INR 500,000 | 10,000 |
| Credit cards with a limit above INR 500,000 | 25,000 |

After 7 working days liability is decided case by case by the disputes team.
