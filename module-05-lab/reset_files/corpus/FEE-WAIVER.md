# Charge reversals and waivers: delegation of authority

## What an agent may do
<!-- section: agent -->

A contact centre agent may not reverse or waive any charge. The agent records the request with the charge code and the customer's reason and passes it to the team lead queue. The customer is told that a decision will be sent within 2 working days.

## Team lead authority
<!-- section: teamlead -->

A team lead may reverse charges of up to INR 1,000 for a customer in a quarter, using waiver code WVR-1 for a first request and WVR-2 for a bank error. A late payment charge on a credit card may be reversed once in 12 months for a cardholder with no other late payment in that period.

## Above team lead authority
<!-- section: above -->

Requests above INR 1,000, and all requests to waive an annual card fee, go to the branch manager or the card retention desk, who may approve up to INR 10,000 using code WVR-3.
