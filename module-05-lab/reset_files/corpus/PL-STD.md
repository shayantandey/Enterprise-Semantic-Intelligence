# Lakemont Personal Loan: product policy

## Eligibility and amount
<!-- section: eligibility -->

Salaried individuals aged 21 to 60 with net monthly income of INR 25,000 or more can apply. Loans range from INR 50,000 to INR 2,500,000 for 12 to 60 months.

## Interest rate and processing fee
<!-- section: rates -->

The rate is fixed for the life of the loan, between 10.75 and 16.50 percent a year depending on the credit score and employer category. The processing fee is 2 percent of the loan amount, up to INR 25,000, and is deducted from the disbursal.

## Prepayment
<!-- section: prepayment -->

The loan can be repaid in full after 12 instalments have been paid. 

Table: prepayment charge as a percentage of principal outstanding
| Instalments paid | Charge |
|---|---|
| 13 to 24 | 4 percent |
| 25 to 36 | 3 percent |
| more than 36 | 2 percent |

Part-prepayment is allowed once a year, up to 25 percent of the principal outstanding, without charge.

## Missed instalments
<!-- section: bounce -->

When an instalment is returned unpaid by the customer's bank, an EMI return charge of INR 590 is applied for each return. Penal interest of 2 percent a month accrues on the overdue instalment until it is paid.

## Documents
<!-- section: documents -->

Identity and address proof, salary slips for 3 months and bank statements for 6 months are required. No security or guarantor is taken.
