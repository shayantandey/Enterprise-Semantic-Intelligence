# Festive Deposit Offer: terms

## The offer
<!-- section: offer -->

During the offer period a deposit booked for exactly 444 days earns 7.40 percent a year, and senior citizens earn 7.90 percent. The minimum deposit is INR 25,000.

## Offer period
<!-- section: period -->

The offer is open for deposits booked from 1 October 2025 to 31 January 2026.

## Premature withdrawal
<!-- section: premature -->

A deposit under the offer that is withdrawn before maturity earns the ordinary card rate for the period it ran, less a penalty of 1.00 percentage point.
