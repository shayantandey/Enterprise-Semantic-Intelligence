# Safe deposit lockers: policy

## Annual rent
<!-- section: rent -->

Table: annual locker rent by size and branch location, INR
| Size | Metro and urban | Semi-urban and rural |
|---|---|---|
| Small | 2,000 | 1,500 |
| Medium | 4,000 | 3,000 |
| Large | 8,000 | 6,000 |
| Extra large | 12,000 | 9,000 |

Rent is collected in advance each April. A security deposit equal to 3 years' rent is taken as a fixed deposit at allotment.

## Overdue rent
<!-- section: overdue -->

A penalty of 10 percent of the annual rent is added for each quarter the rent is overdue. If rent is unpaid for 3 years the bank may open the locker after 3 written notices, in the presence of two witnesses.

## Access and lost keys
<!-- section: access -->

The locker can be operated during branch hours by the hirers named in the agreement. If the key is lost the hirer pays INR 1,500 plus the locksmith's actual cost to have the locker opened and the lock changed.
