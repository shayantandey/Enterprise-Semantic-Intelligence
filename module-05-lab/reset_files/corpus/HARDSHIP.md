# Borrowers in financial difficulty: collections policy

## Relief options
<!-- section: options -->

A borrower who has lost income can be offered a moratorium of up to 3 months on instalments, with interest continuing to accrue, or an extension of the loan term by up to 24 months to bring the instalment down. Both need proof of the loss of income.

## Settlement authority
<!-- section: settlement -->

A collections officer may agree a one-time settlement that waives penal interest and charges in full. Any waiver of principal needs the approval of the regional credit head.

## Conduct
<!-- section: conduct -->

Calls to borrowers are made only between 8 in the morning and 7 in the evening. Family members, employers and referees are not told about the debt.
