# Lakemont Fixed Deposit: product policy

## Eligibility and amount
<!-- section: eligibility -->

A fixed deposit can be opened by any savings or current account holder. The minimum deposit is INR 10,000 and there is no maximum. Deposits of INR 30,000,000 and above are bulk deposits and are quoted by the treasury desk, not from the card rates below.

## Tenure and interest rates
<!-- section: rates -->

Table: card rates for deposits below INR 30,000,000, percent a year
| Tenure | Rate |
|---|---|
| 7 to 45 days | 3.50 |
| 46 to 179 days | 5.00 |
| 180 to 364 days | 6.00 |
| 1 year to less than 2 years | 6.80 |
| 2 years to less than 5 years | 7.00 |
| 5 years to 10 years | 6.75 |

The rate on the date of booking applies for the full tenure. Interest is paid monthly, quarterly or at maturity as the depositor chooses.

## Premature withdrawal
<!-- section: premature -->

A deposit can be withdrawn before maturity on a request made with Form FD-7. Interest is then paid at the card rate that applied on the booking date for the period the deposit actually ran, less a penalty of 1.00 percentage point. No interest is paid if the deposit is withdrawn within 7 days of booking.

Partial withdrawal is allowed in multiples of INR 1,000, and the remaining amount continues at the original rate.

## Loan against the deposit
<!-- section: loan -->

The depositor can borrow up to 90 percent of the deposit amount. The loan is charged at 2.00 percentage points above the deposit rate and must be repaid on or before the maturity date. No processing fee is charged.

## Maturity and renewal
<!-- section: renewal -->

Unless the depositor gives other instructions, the deposit is renewed on maturity for the same tenure at the card rate on that day. A deposit that is neither renewed nor claimed earns the savings account rate from the maturity date.

## Tax deducted at source
<!-- section: tax -->

Tax is deducted when interest across all the customer's deposits is above INR 50,000 in a financial year, or INR 100,000 for senior citizens. Form 15G or 15H, where the customer is eligible, must be given at the start of each financial year.
