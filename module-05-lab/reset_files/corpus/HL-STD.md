# Lakemont Home Loan: product policy

## Eligibility and amount
<!-- section: eligibility -->

Salaried and self-employed individuals aged 21 to 65 at loan maturity can apply. The loan can be up to 80 percent of the property value for loans above INR 3,000,000 and up to 90 percent for smaller loans, for a term of up to 30 years.

## Interest rate and processing fee
<!-- section: rates -->

Floating-rate loans are linked to the repo rate and are currently priced between 8.60 and 9.75 percent a year. A fixed rate of 10.25 percent is available for the first 3 years. The processing fee is 0.50 percent of the loan amount, up to INR 15,000.

## Prepayment
<!-- section: prepayment -->

No charge is levied on prepayment of a floating-rate loan taken by an individual, in part or in full, at any time. On a fixed-rate loan a charge of 2 percent of the amount prepaid applies when the money comes from another lender. Each part-prepayment must be at least twice the instalment amount.

## Missed instalments
<!-- section: bounce -->

An EMI return charge of INR 500 is applied for each returned instalment. Penal interest of 2 percent a month accrues on the overdue amount.

## Top-up loan
<!-- section: topup -->

A borrower with 12 on-time instalments can take a top-up loan of up to INR 2,500,000 at 0.50 percentage points above the home loan rate, provided total borrowing stays within the permitted share of the property value.
