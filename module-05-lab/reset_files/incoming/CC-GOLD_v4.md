# Lakemont Gold Credit Card: product policy

## Eligibility
<!-- section: eligibility -->

The Lakemont Gold Credit Card is offered to individuals aged 21 to 65 with a credit bureau score of 720 or above. Income documents are not needed from customers whose salary has been credited to a Lakemont account for 6 months.

## Annual fee
<!-- section: fees -->

There is no joining fee. The annual fee is INR 1,499, charged in the statement after each card anniversary. It is reversed when spend in the previous card year was INR 150,000 or more. Add-on cards are free.

## Interest-free period and finance charges
<!-- section: grace -->

Purchases have an interest-free period of up to 50 days when the previous statement was paid in full by the due date. If any amount is left unpaid, a finance charge of 3.40 percent a month applies to every purchase from its transaction date until it is repaid. Cash withdrawals carry the finance charge from the day of withdrawal and a cash advance fee of 2.5 percent, minimum INR 500.

## Late payment
<!-- section: late -->

A late payment charge is applied when the minimum amount due is not received by the payment due date. The charge depends on the total amount due on the statement.

Table: late payment charge by total amount due
| Total amount due (INR) | Charge (INR) |
|---|---|
| up to 500 | nil |
| 501 to 5,000 | 500 |
| 5,001 to 20,000 | 900 |
| 20,001 to 50,000 | 1,200 |
| above 50,000 | 1,300 |

The charge is not applied if payment is received within 3 days after the due date. A missed payment is reported to credit bureaus only after that period.

## Lost or stolen card
<!-- section: lost -->

The cardholder must report the loss at once on the 24-hour helpline or in the mobile app, and the card is hotlisted immediately. The cardholder is not liable for any transaction made after the report. A replacement card is sent within 7 working days and costs INR 200.

## Rewards and benefits
<!-- section: rewards -->

The card earns 2 reward points for every INR 150 spent, except on fuel, rent and wallet loads. It gives 4 complimentary domestic airport lounge visits in a calendar year. The foreign currency mark-up is 3.50 percent of the transaction amount.

## Credit limit
<!-- section: limit -->

The limit is reviewed every 6 months. The cardholder can ask for an increase after 6 on-time payments, with the latest salary slip or income tax return. From version 4 of this policy, a temporary limit increase of up to 20 percent for 45 days can be given on the phone to cardholders with 12 on-time payments.
