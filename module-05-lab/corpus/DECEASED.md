# Settling the accounts of a deceased customer: process

## Where there is a nominee or a surviving joint holder
<!-- section: nominee -->

The balance is paid to the nominee or the survivor on receipt of Form DC-1, the death certificate and the claimant's own identity proof. No succession certificate or indemnity is asked for.

## Where there is no nominee
<!-- section: nonominee -->

Claims of up to INR 500,000 are settled on Form DC-1 with the death certificate, a letter of disclaimer from the other legal heirs and an indemnity. Above that amount a succession certificate or probate is required.

## Time to settle
<!-- section: timeline -->

A claim is settled within 15 days of receiving complete documents. Fixed deposits of the deceased can be closed before maturity without penalty.
