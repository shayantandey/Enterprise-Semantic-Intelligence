# Lakemont Tax Saver Deposit: product policy

## Eligibility and amount
<!-- section: eligibility -->

The Tax Saver Deposit is open to resident individuals and Hindu undivided families with a PAN. The minimum is INR 100 and the maximum is INR 150,000 in a financial year across all such deposits. A joint deposit gives the tax benefit only to the first holder.

## Tenure and interest rate
<!-- section: rates -->

The tenure is fixed at 5 years and the rate is 6.75 percent a year, set on the booking date. Interest is paid quarterly or at maturity.

## Lock-in
<!-- section: premature -->

The deposit has a lock-in of 5 years. It cannot be withdrawn before maturity, in whole or in part, and no penalty option exists. The only exception is the death of the depositor, when the nominee or legal heir may claim the amount using Form FD-9.

## Loans and pledges
<!-- section: loan -->

No loan is given against this deposit and it cannot be pledged as security during the lock-in.

## Maturity
<!-- section: renewal -->

The deposit is not renewed automatically. On maturity the amount is credited to the linked savings account.
