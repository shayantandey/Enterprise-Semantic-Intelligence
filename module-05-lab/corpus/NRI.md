# NRE and NRO accounts: policy

## Account types
<!-- section: types -->

A non-resident Indian can hold an NRE account, funded from abroad and freely repatriable, and an NRO account, for income earned in India such as rent or dividends. Both can be savings accounts or fixed deposits.

## Sending money abroad
<!-- section: repatriation -->

The balance in an NRE account, with interest, can be sent abroad without limit. From an NRO account up to USD 1,000,000 can be sent abroad in a financial year after tax, with a chartered accountant's certificate in Form 15CB.

## Tax
<!-- section: tax -->

Interest on an NRE account is exempt from Indian income tax. Interest on an NRO account has tax deducted at 30 percent plus surcharge and cess, unless a tax treaty rate applies and the customer has given a tax residency certificate.

## Change of residential status
<!-- section: status -->

A customer who returns to India for good must tell the bank within 30 days. NRE accounts are then redesignated as resident accounts, or moved to a resident foreign currency account if the customer asks.
