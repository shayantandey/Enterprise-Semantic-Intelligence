# Periodic KYC update: process

## How often
<!-- section: frequency -->

Customer records are updated every 2 years for high-risk customers, every 8 years for medium-risk customers and every 10 years for low-risk customers. The risk category is shown on the customer profile screen.

## How the customer can update
<!-- section: method -->

A customer whose details have not changed can confirm this by self-declaration in the mobile app, in internet banking, or by a letter to the branch. A change of address needs a fresh officially valid document, which can be uploaded in the app and is verified within 2 working days.

## When the update is overdue
<!-- section: overdue -->

Three reminders are sent, starting 60 days before the due date. If the update is not done within 30 days after the due date, debits from the account are restricted. Credits continue. The restriction is lifted on the same day the documents are accepted.
