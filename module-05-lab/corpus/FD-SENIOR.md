# Lakemont Senior Citizen Deposit: product policy

## Eligibility
<!-- section: eligibility -->

The deposit is for resident individuals aged 60 or above on the booking date. In a joint deposit the senior citizen must be the first holder. Proof of age is taken once and recorded on the customer profile.

## Interest rates
<!-- section: rates -->

The depositor earns 0.50 percentage points above the card rate of the Lakemont Fixed Deposit for the same tenure. Depositors aged 80 or above earn 0.75 percentage points above the card rate. The additional rate applies to deposits of 1 year and longer.

## Premature withdrawal
<!-- section: premature -->

A request is made with Form FD-7S. The penalty is 0.50 percentage points, and it is waived in full when the withdrawal is for the depositor's medical treatment and a hospital estimate is attached.

## Loan against the deposit
<!-- section: loan -->

The depositor can borrow up to 90 percent of the deposit amount at 1.00 percentage point above the deposit rate.

## Maturity and renewal
<!-- section: renewal -->

The deposit is renewed for the same tenure unless instructed otherwise. The additional rate continues on renewal.
