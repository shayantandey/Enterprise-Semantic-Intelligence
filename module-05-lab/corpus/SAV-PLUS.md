# Lakemont Plus Savings Account: product policy

## Eligibility
<!-- section: eligibility -->

The Lakemont Plus Savings Account can be opened by a resident individual aged 18 or above, singly or jointly. Salaried customers whose employer has a payroll arrangement with the bank are exempt from the balance requirement while salary is credited. A valid PAN or Form 60 and one officially valid document for address are required at account opening.

## Balance requirement
<!-- section: balance -->

The account must hold a monthly average balance of INR 10,000 at metro and urban branches and INR 5,000 at semi-urban and rural branches. The monthly average balance is the sum of end-of-day balances divided by the number of days in the month.

If the requirement is not met, a non-maintenance charge of INR 300 is debited in the first week of the following month. The customer is sent an SMS and an email at least 30 days before the first charge is applied, and the charge is never applied so as to take the balance below zero.

## Service charges
<!-- section: charges -->

Table: service charges for the account
| Charge code | Service | Charge (INR) |
|---|---|---|
| CHG-111 | Cheque book of 25 leaves, after the first free book each year | 50 |
| CHG-112 | Duplicate statement, per request | 75 |
| CHG-113 | Demand draft, per instrument | 40 |
| CHG-114 | Stop payment instruction, per cheque | 75 |
| CHG-115 | Replacement of a damaged debit card | 200 |

Goods and services tax is added to every charge in the table. Charges are debited on the day of the service.

## Interest
<!-- section: interest -->

Interest is paid at 3.00 percent a year on the whole balance. Interest is calculated on the daily closing balance and credited at the end of every quarter.

## Debit card and ATM use
<!-- section: card -->

The account comes with a Gold debit card. The annual card fee of INR 250 is waived when card spend in the previous year was above INR 50,000. The holder gets 8 free transactions a month at other banks' ATMs, after which each financial transaction costs INR 21 and each non-financial transaction INR 10. Transactions at Lakemont ATMs are free.

## Closing the account
<!-- section: closure -->

A closure charge of INR 500 applies if the account is closed within 12 months of opening. There is no charge in the first 14 days or after 12 months. The request must be signed by all holders. Unused cheque leaves and the debit card are surrendered, and the balance is paid by transfer or demand draft, never in cash above INR 20,000.

## Inoperative accounts
<!-- section: dormancy -->

The account is classified as inoperative when there has been no customer-initiated transaction for 24 months. Interest continues to be credited. To reactivate it the customer visits any branch with fresh KYC documents and makes one transaction. No charge is levied for reactivation.
