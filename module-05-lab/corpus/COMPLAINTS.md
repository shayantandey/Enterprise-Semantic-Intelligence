# Complaint handling and escalation: process

## Recording a complaint
<!-- section: record -->

Every expression of dissatisfaction is recorded as a complaint in the service system at first contact, including those resolved on the call. The customer is given the complaint number by SMS.

## Escalation levels
<!-- section: levels -->

Table: escalation levels and the time allowed at each
| Level | Handled by | Time allowed |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Contact centre or branch | 3 working days |
| 2 | Nodal officer of the region | 7 working days |
| 3 | Principal nodal officer | 10 working days |

A customer who has had no final reply in 30 days from the first complaint, or is not satisfied with it, may approach the banking ombudsman. The agent must say so when the customer asks about further options.

## Compensation
<!-- section: compensation -->

Where the bank is at fault, charges wrongly applied are reversed with interest at the savings rate. Compensation beyond the reversal is decided at level 2.
