# UPI transaction limits: policy

## Standard limits
<!-- section: limits -->

A customer can send up to INR 100,000 in a single UPI transaction and up to INR 100,000 in total in 24 hours, in not more than 20 transactions. Payments for tax, initial public offers, hospitals and educational institutions have a limit of INR 500,000 per transaction.

## After registering on a new device
<!-- section: newdevice -->

For the first 24 hours after UPI is registered on a new phone or with a new SIM, the customer can send no more than INR 5,000 in total. This cooling period cannot be removed by an agent.

## Failed transactions
<!-- section: failed -->

If money is debited and the payee is not credited, the amount is returned automatically within 1 working day. The customer is paid INR 100 a day for any delay beyond that.
