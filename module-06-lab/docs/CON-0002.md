# Contract summary: Northfield Actives

## Term and termination
<!-- section: term -->

The agreement with Northfield Actives renews each year. Either party may terminate with 90 days' written notice. The early termination fee is EUR 200 thousand.

## Price
<!-- section: price -->

Paracetamol and insulin glargine are priced per kilogram under a volume band table.
