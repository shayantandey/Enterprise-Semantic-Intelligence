# Supplier qualification and audit policy

## Audit frequency
<!-- section: frequency -->

Suppliers of active ingredients are audited every 12 months and suppliers of excipients and packaging components every 24 months. A supplier with an open critical finding is audited again within 6 months.

## Severity of findings
<!-- section: severity -->

A critical finding is one that could put patients at risk or shows that data cannot be trusted. A major finding is a significant departure from good manufacturing practice without immediate patient risk. A minor finding is an isolated lapse.

## Remediation
<!-- section: remediation -->

The supplier submits a corrective action plan within 15 days for a critical finding and within 30 days for a major finding. A critical finding is expected to be closed within 60 days, after which the supplier termination policy applies.

## GMP certificate
<!-- section: certificate -->

A supplier must hold a valid GMP certificate from a recognised authority at all times. Purchase orders are blocked from the day a certificate lapses.
