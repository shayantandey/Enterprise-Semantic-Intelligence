# Revenue definitions for management reporting

## Net revenue
<!-- section: net -->

Net revenue is the gross invoiced amount, less credit notes issued for returned goods, less rebate accruals. It is reported in thousands of euros. Version 2 of the definition, in force from 1 January 2026, deducts rebates when they are accrued and no longer when they are paid.

## Sales regions
<!-- section: regions -->

Management reporting uses four sales regions. Europe is the member states of the European Union, the United Kingdom and Switzerland. The other regions are India, North America and Rest of world. The ERP stores a sales region code on each invoice that separates the European Union, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, so a report that filters on one code does not cover Europe.

## Authoritative source
<!-- section: source -->

The metrics layer is the only approved source of net revenue. Figures taken from invoice tables directly are not to be used in board papers.
