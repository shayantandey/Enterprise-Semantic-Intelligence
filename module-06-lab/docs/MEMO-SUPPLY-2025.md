# Supply base overview for the 2025 plant reviews

## Ahmedabad injectables plant
<!-- section: ahmedabad -->

Heparin sodium for the Ahmedabad plant comes from Baltic Synthesis and Zenith Actives. Paracetamol comes from Northfield Actives, and glass vials from Meridian Glass.

## Hyderabad oral solids plant
<!-- section: hyderabad -->

Amoxicillin for Hyderabad comes from Kaveri Fine Chemicals. Paracetamol is supplied by Northfield Actives and Sagar Pharma Ingredients.
