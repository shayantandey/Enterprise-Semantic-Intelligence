# Dual sourcing standard

## The standard
<!-- section: rule -->

Every component used in a critical product must have two qualified suppliers for each plant that uses it. A position with one supplier is a sole-source position and is recorded in the supply risk register.

## Time-limited exceptions
<!-- section: exceptions -->

A sole-source position is allowed for up to 12 months while a second supplier is being qualified, with the approval of the Head of Quality.
