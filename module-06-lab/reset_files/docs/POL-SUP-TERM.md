# Supplier termination policy

## Purpose and scope
<!-- section: purpose -->

This policy sets out when Corvane Pharma may end a supply agreement for cause, who decides and how much notice is given. It applies to every supplier of active ingredients, excipients and packaging components to a Corvane plant.

## Grounds for termination
<!-- section: grounds -->

A supplier is in breach of this policy, and its agreements may be terminated for cause, when any one of three conditions holds. Delivery: the supplier's on-time-in-full delivery rate is below 90 percent in two consecutive quarters. Quality: a critical audit finding has been open for more than 60 days. Certification: the supplier's GMP certificate has lapsed or has been suspended.

## Notice
<!-- section: notice -->

Termination for cause is given with 90 days' written notice. Where the supplier is the only qualified source of a component for a plant, the notice is 180 days and a supply continuity plan must be approved before notice is served.

## Who decides
<!-- section: approval -->

The Chief Procurement Officer approves every termination for cause. Termination of a supplier of a component used in a critical product also needs the approval of the Head of Quality.

## Exceptions
<!-- section: exceptions -->

A breach on delivery is waived for a quarter in which the supplier's shortfall was caused by a force majeure event accepted in writing by Group Procurement.
