# Procurement approval limits

## Approval limits
<!-- section: limits -->

A purchase order of up to EUR 50 thousand is approved by the category buyer, up to EUR 500 thousand by the Head of Category, and above that by the Chief Procurement Officer. Orders above EUR 2,000 thousand also need the approval of the Chief Financial Officer.

## Supplier concentration
<!-- section: concentration -->

A supplier that accounts for more than 10 percent of procurement spend over the last four quarters is a strategic supplier. Any decision to end or materially change the relationship with a strategic supplier is reported to the executive committee.
