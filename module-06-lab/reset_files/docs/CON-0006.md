# Contract summary: Baltic Synthesis

## Term and termination
<!-- section: term -->

The master supply agreement with Baltic Synthesis runs to 31 March 2029. Either party may terminate with 120 days' written notice. Corvane pays an early termination fee of EUR 450 thousand if it terminates without cause before 31 March 2027.

## Price
<!-- section: price -->

Heparin sodium is priced per million international units, with an annual adjustment capped at 4 percent.
