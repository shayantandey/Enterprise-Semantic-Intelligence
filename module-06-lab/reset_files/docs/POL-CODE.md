# Supplier code of conduct

## Labour standards
<!-- section: labour -->

Suppliers must not use forced or child labour and must pay at least the legal minimum wage.

## Gifts and hospitality
<!-- section: gifts -->

Corvane staff may not accept gifts from suppliers worth more than EUR 50 in a year. Every offer above that value is reported to the compliance office.

## Reporting concerns
<!-- section: reporting -->

Concerns about a supplier's conduct are reported to the compliance office, which acknowledges each report within 5 working days.
