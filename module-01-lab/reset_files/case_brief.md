# Case brief: the retailer (fictional, written for this course)

- 310 stores, an e-commerce site and a mobile app. Listed company.
- Four domains use customer data: marketing, finance, supply chain, customer service.
- Data team: a central data engineering team of nine. Marketing has three analysts, finance has two.
  Supply chain has two data engineers of its own. Customer service has no data staff.
- Systems: CRM, ERP, and a customer master data hub that covers about 60 percent of customers.
- Reporting: statutory accounts, and a quarterly investor presentation that quotes active customers and net revenue.
- Change requests: the central team takes about three weeks to deliver a change to a shared model.
  Marketing asks for definition changes about twice a month.
- The dispute: marketing, finance and customer service each report a different number of active customers.
